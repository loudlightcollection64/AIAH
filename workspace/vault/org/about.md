# About So Many Angels

## Observations
- [identity] 501(c)(3) nonprofit; founder and Executive Director is Matt Meiers (email@somanyangels.org)
- [mission] Provide hope, inspiration and encouragement to children facing cancer and other serious illnesses
- [motto] "Make Hope Visible"
- [service] Each child gets a professional photo session in a costume or theme of their choosing, edited into cinematic artwork
- [service] Each family receives a 16×24 printed canvas; standard experience has also included a So Many Angels blanket and sometimes a 60×80 tribute blanket
- [cost] Free to families; estimated cost to serve one child is $350
- [scale] 375+ children photographed; goal of 300 more by the end of 2027
- [locations] Events have run in Pittsburgh, Kansas City (Lee's Summit) and Indianapolis
- [partners] HopeKids (Kansas City canvas presentations), Dimensions Photography (Indianapolis venue), Costumes for Courage (costume help), H&H Color Lab (printing)
- [fundraising] Donations and sponsorships run through Givebutter; sponsorship tiers have included $3,500 / $1,750 / $1,050 / $350 / $250
- [future] Affiliate photographer programme: local volunteer photographers each serving two children
