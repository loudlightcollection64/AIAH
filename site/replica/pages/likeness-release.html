Before You Sign Up

There is no charge for this. Every family receives a photo session and a 16×24 printed canvas, shipped to the address on your form, allow up to 60 days for delivery, unless a canvas reveal event is planned.

On photo day, a volunteer will meet you at check-in and walk you to your station. Sit back and let your kid be a kid, that moment's theirs.

After your canvas is delivered, you'll have the opportunity to purchase additional canvas prints and unedited photos, which helps us continue serving families like yours.

A note about costumes: You're responsible for bringing your child's costume, and there will be space to change on-site. If you need help, reach out to Costumes for Courage right away with your session date.
